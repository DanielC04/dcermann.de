Welcome to the new version of my personal site!

In the last weeks I have started enjoying reading papers about topics that I'm intersted in. Rather than just reading and forgetting abou them, I decided to start collecting thoughts and ideas on them. This way I will think about the papers more deeply and also have a place to build a collection of knowledge that I can refer back to in the future. My personal website will be the place of choice for this.
This is why I decided to rebuild my personal homepage from scratch with **Astro** — a framework purpose-built for content-driven, mostly-static sites.

## Why Astro?

A few things sold me on Astro:

- **Zero JS by default.** Pages ship as static HTML. Client-side JS is only added where genuinely needed.
- **Content collections.** Markdown files with typed frontmatter — perfect for a blog.
- **SCSS support out of the box.** I already had an extensive set of SCSS styles I wanted to keep.
- **Easy migration path.** I could preserve all my content (projects, skills, experience) and just rebuild the components.

## What to expect here

I plan to write about things I'm reading, building and topics I come across while studying at HPI, and the occasional longer-form technical deep-dive. No fixed schedule — just whenever I have something worth saying.

Thanks for stopping by.
